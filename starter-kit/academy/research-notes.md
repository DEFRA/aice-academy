# Research notes (fictional)

> **Practice material.** These notes are invented for the AICE Academy. They describe made-up people.

We spoke to 8 people who had seen hedgehogs in the last month, and to 2 people from the nature recovery team.

## Members of the public

- **Most saw a hedgehog at night, in their garden or on a road.** Several said they reported it "the next morning", so the date they remember is not always the day they report.
- **Nearly everyone used their phone.** Two said they gave up on a previous form because "it was tiny on my phone and kept zooming in".
- **People don't know their exact location in the dark.** A street name or postcode felt fine. Nobody wanted to drop a pin on a map.
- **People worry about injured hedgehogs.** Three asked "what should I do if it's hurt?" They wanted advice straight away, not just a form.
- **Mixed feelings about giving an email address.** Some liked the idea of a thank you; others said "only if it's optional".
- **One person uses a screen reader.** They said forms with lots of questions on one page are "exhausting" and they often lose their place.

## Nature recovery team

- "The thing we need most is location and date. Without those, a report is almost useless."
- "If it's injured, we want to know quickly so we can follow up."
- "Counting is tricky. People say 'a few'. A rough number is fine."
- "We have to be careful with personal data. We only want an email if they've chosen to give it."
