# Service brief: Report a hedgehog sighting

> **Practice service.** This brief is for the AICE Academy only. It is not a real Defra service. Use made-up data.

## The problem

Hedgehog numbers in Britain have fallen. A (fictional) nature recovery team wants to know where people are seeing hedgehogs, so they can plan "hedgehog highways": small gaps in garden fences and walls that let hedgehogs travel between gardens.

At the moment, sightings arrive by email in every possible format. The team spends hours tidying them up, and important details like the date or whether the hedgehog was injured are often missing.

## What the service needs to do

Let a member of the public report a hedgehog sighting in under two minutes, on their phone, with the details the team needs.

## What the team needs to know

- where the hedgehog was seen (a town, street or postcode is fine)
- when it was seen
- how many hedgehogs were seen
- whether it looked healthy or injured
- optionally, an email address so the team can send a thank you and updates

## What success looks like

- most people finish their report without help
- every report has a location, a date and a condition
- injured hedgehog reports are flagged so the team can point people to a rescue centre

## Out of scope for the first version

- photo uploads
- maps
- accounts or sign in
- reporting other animals
