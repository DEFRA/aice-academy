# Show and tell

Read `backlog.md`, `TEAM_LOG.md`, `quality-report.md` and `design/journey.md`. Then write `show-and-tell.md` with:

1. **The service in one sentence**: who it's for and what it does.
2. **Demo script**: the exact clicks for a five-minute demo, including one deliberate mistake to show an error message.
3. **What we built**: pages, tests (count and what they cover), and quality results.
4. **How the team worked**: which agents did what, two moments where the golden loop caught a problem, and one decision only a human could make.
5. **What I learned**: three prompts for the learner to fill in themselves. Do not fill them in.
6. **What's next**: stories in "Later", and one idea for using this way of working in the learner's real job.

Keep it to one page. Plain English.
