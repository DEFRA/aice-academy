## What you're about to do

You'll start **Claude Code**: Claude working inside your codespace, where it can read and change your service's files. It always asks before doing anything important.

## Do this

1. Open a second terminal: in the terminal panel, select the **+** button on the right.
2. Copy this, paste it into the new terminal and press **Enter**:

```bash
claude
```

3. Claude asks you a few questions the first time. Use the arrow keys to choose and press **Enter**:
   - **Text style:** choose any. It only changes colours.
   - **How to sign in:** choose the option to sign in with your Claude account.
4. Claude shows a sign-in link. Open it, sign in with the account your facilitator gave you, then come back to your codespace.
5. If Claude asks whether you trust the files in this folder, choose **Yes**. They're the practice files you just created.
